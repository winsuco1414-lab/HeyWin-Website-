Questions about funnels and websites

How much does it cost to build a funnel?

A basic lead generation (opt-in) funnel is $300–$500 USD. A standard sales or course funnel is $750–$1,500 USD. Both are one-time. After launch, you can add the Care Plan for $120 USD a month.

How long does it take to build a funnel?

A basic lead generation funnel takes 2–4 days. A standard sales or course funnel takes 7–10 days.

Which funnel platforms do you build in?

GoHighLevel, Systeme.io or ClickFunnels.

What happens after my funnel goes live?

You can add the optional Care Plan. I keep your funnel converting: A/B testing, conversion fixes, automation tweaks, reporting and tech support. It’s month-to-month.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to get started, and the balance when the project is finished.

I accept PayPal and Wise, and all prices are in USD.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. Funnels and websites are a one-time project, and the Care Plan runs month-to-month.

Do you take small or one-off tasks?

Yes. If you need help with something outside the packages, mention it on your call and I’ll give you a quote.
