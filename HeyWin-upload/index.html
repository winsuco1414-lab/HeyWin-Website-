Social media management questions, answered

How much does a social media manager cost?

With me, Starter is $399 USD a month for 2 platforms and 3 posts a week. Growth is $799 USD a month for up to 4 platforms and 5 posts a week, plus a full content strategy and a monthly strategy review call. Both are month-to-month. See what’s included

Freelance social media manager or an agency?

When you work with me, you work with me. No hand-offs, no account managers, no guessing who’s looking after your brand. And because one person handles your content, your funnel and your follow-up, they’re built to work together.

How many posts a week will I get?

Starter includes 3 posts a week: 1 reel plus 2 carousels or static posts. Growth includes 5 posts a week: 3 reels plus 2 carousels or static posts.

What are your working hours?

My hours are flexible. We agree on working hours that suit your time zone, and during those hours I usually reply within minutes.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. Starter, Growth and the Care Plan all run month-to-month. Funnels and websites are a one-time project.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to get started. For monthly plans, the balance is due on the 15th of the month. For funnels and websites, the balance is due when the project is finished.

I accept PayPal and Wise, and all prices are in USD.

How soon will I see results?

You’ll see consistency from the first month: a steady, planned posting rhythm. Growth in reach and leads builds over the months that follow, and it varies by business. That’s why I report on it every month, so you always know where things stand.

Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and Threads. Starter covers 2 platforms, and Growth covers up to 4.

What do you need from me?

A short onboarding chat, access to your accounts, your brand assets (logo, colours, any photos or videos), and quick approvals when content is ready. I handle the rest.

Do you take small or one-off tasks?

Yes. If you need help with something outside the packages, mention it on your call and I’ll give you a quote.

Do you reply to comments and DMs, or run paid ads?

These aren’t part of the standard packages. If you need them, ask on your call and I’ll tell you what’s possible.

How much does it cost to build a funnel?

A basic lead generation (opt-in) funnel is $300–$500 USD and takes 2–4 days. A standard sales or course funnel is $750–$1,500 USD and takes 7–10 days. Both are one-time, and you can add the Care Plan for $120 USD a month after launch. More about funnels

Which funnel platforms do you build in?

GoHighLevel, Systeme.io or ClickFunnels.

Can you automate my DMs and follow-up emails?

Yes. I set up email sequences and DM automation with ManyChat and GoHighLevel, so every lead hears back, even on your busiest days. This is separate from replying to comments and DMs by hand. More about automation

What happens after my funnel goes live?

You can add the optional Care Plan for $120 USD a month. I keep your funnel converting: A/B testing, conversion fixes, automation tweaks, reporting and tech support. It’s month-to-month.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. I’m based in the Philippines and work with clients around the world. We agree on working hours that suit your time zone.

What happens on the discovery call?

It’s a free, no-pressure 30-minute chat. We talk about your business, your goals and what’s getting in the way. You’ll leave knowing what I’d do first, whether or not we work together.
