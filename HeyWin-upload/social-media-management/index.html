Questions about social media management

How much does a social media manager cost?

With me, Starter is $399 USD a month for 2 platforms and 3 posts a week. Growth is $799 USD a month for up to 4 platforms and 5 posts a week, plus a full content strategy and a monthly strategy review call. Both are month-to-month.

Freelance social media manager or an agency?

When you work with me, you work with me. No hand-offs, no account managers, no guessing who’s looking after your brand.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. Starter and Growth both run month-to-month.

How soon will I see results?

You’ll see consistency from the first month: a steady, planned posting rhythm. Growth in reach and leads builds over the months that follow, and it varies by business. That’s why I report on it every month, so you always know where things stand.

What are your working hours?

My hours are flexible. We agree on working hours that suit your time zone, and during those hours I usually reply within minutes.

Do you reply to comments and DMs, or run paid ads?

These aren’t part of the standard packages. If you need them, ask on your call and I’ll tell you what’s possible. If you want DMs to get an instant reply, see automation.
